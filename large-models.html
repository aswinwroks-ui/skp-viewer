Large SketchUp models.
Processed on your PC.

Use local conversion for models too large to parse in a browser. Your SKP file and converted geometry stay on your computer.

Download Windows local viewer
  1. Extract the ZIP, then open the local folder.
  2. Install 64-bit Python 3.10 or newer if needed, with “Add Python to PATH” enabled.
  3. Double-click Start Viewer.cmd and choose your SKP file. First launch downloads converter dependencies.
  4. Wait for conversion. The local viewer opens automatically. Keep the command window open.
  5. Choose model parts using Previous, Next, or the part selector. Drag to rotate.

What to expect

One part is visible at a time to limit browser memory. This is a preparation workflow for large files, not verified 2 GB compatibility. Conversion still needs enough PC RAM and disk space; complex models may require tens of GB of memory. A single very large component can exceed the limits even when split by placements.

Model colors and placements are preserved. Nested hierarchy is flattened; textures and loose edges are not included. Internet access downloads rendering libraries, but model data is served only from your PC.
